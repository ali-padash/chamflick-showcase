German reading, in colour

See the gender. Remember the word.

ChamFlick DE colours every German noun by its gender while you read: in your own books, in scanned pages and in film subtitles. Tap any word for its meaning. It all runs on the phone.

The colour key

Four colours. Each one only ever means a gender.

After a few pages the colour arrives before the article does. That is the point: you stop looking genders up and start remembering them.

dermasculineTisch · Sommer · Kopf
diefeminineGabel · Freundin · Höhe
dasneuterMesser · Mädchen · Bett
diepluralTräume · Beine · Bücher

A noun the app is not sure of gets no colour, only a dashed underline. A wrong colour would read as a confident answer, so it does not guess.

Read

Your books, your scans, your films.

Add a PDF or an EPUB from the phone and start reading while the rest is still being coloured. Photographed pages are read by OCR. Subtitles are coloured as the film plays.

Understand

It reads the sentence, not just the word.

A German parser works through every sentence: its structure, each word's base form, the cases. That is how a verb split across a sentence is put back together, and how one spelling can come out in three colours.
